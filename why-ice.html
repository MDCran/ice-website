Q1. Why choose International Computer Exchange?

ICE is a proud IBM Business Partner, trusted since 1990. We combine IBM expertise with CloudSAFE and partner solutions—managed cloud, disaster recovery, backup, and technology resale. You know your business; we know technology. Together we create innovative solutions.

Q2. What solutions does ICE offer?

We offer Cloud & Hosting, Disaster Recovery, Backup & Security, and Hardware & Resale. We use and sell solutions from CloudSAFE, Source Data, Midland, Flagship, and DirectSys—purpose-built infrastructure for businesses that can't afford downtime.

Q3. Where is ICE's infrastructure hosted?

ICE's secure hosting infrastructure is built on SOC 1–SSAE 18 Type II certified data centers: a 20,000 sq. ft. facility in Minneapolis, Minnesota, with backup data centers in California. Engineered for AS400, iSeries, and hybrid cloud hosting, with 24/7 monitoring and high-performance cooling, power, and physical security.

Q4. Does ICE resell new hardware?

Yes. We resell new and refurbished hardware—servers, storage, networking—including IBM Power, iSeries, and AS400. Technology procurement made simple. It's not our main focus, but we offer it to support your infrastructure needs.

Q5. How do I get started?

Contact us at sales@icesales.com or 561-394-9188. We'll discuss your needs and match you with the right solutions. Ready to eliminate infrastructure worries? We're here to help.
