SMS / Text Messaging – Opt-In & Opt-Out

International Computer Exchange, Inc. (“ICE”) may send you SMS (text) messages via RingCentral or similar platforms for service updates, appointment reminders, support, and—when you consent—marketing. This page explains how opt-in and opt-out work.

Default Opt-In

When you provide your mobile phone number to International Computer Exchange (e.g., on a form, during a call, or when contacting us), you are default opted in to receive SMS messages from ICE. We may use your number to send service-related messages, project updates, and similar communications.

Opting Out

You may opt out at any time by:

  • Replying STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE to any SMS you receive from us. We will honor opt-out requests promptly.
  • Emailing us at sales@icesales.com and asking to be removed from SMS.
  • Calling us at 561-394-9188 and requesting to be removed from SMS.

After you opt out, we will cease sending you SMS for that purpose. Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier.

How You Can Sign Up

You can sign up for or confirm SMS consent in any of these ways:

  • Express consent on the phone — When you speak with us and provide your mobile number, you may consent to receive texts.
  • Texting our number — Sending a text message to our number to request information or updates.
  • Subscribing on the internet — Using a web form (e.g., “Sign up to receive personalized texts and updates from ICE”) that clearly states you are opting in to SMS.

Frequency & Types of Messages

If you are opted in, you may receive service-related messages (e.g., support, appointments, project updates) and, where you have consented, marketing or promotional messages. We will not send excessive messages.

Carrier Compliance

Our SMS practices follow applicable guidelines, including CTIA messaging guidelines and carrier requirements. We do not share your mobile number or SMS opt-in consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing.

Questions

For questions or to opt out, contact us at sales@icesales.com or 561-394-9188. See our Terms of Service for general website terms.
